# Student Guide

The khgfkjh project is a comprehensive platform leveraging Node.js, MongoDB, Express.js, Vanilla JavaScript, pure CSS, and HTML to enhance the academic experience for students. Its microservices offer essential features such as detailed office information, TA directories, comprehensive course resources, department schedules, grade and GPA calculators, and a centralized gateway for academic resources. These functionalities aim to streamline various aspects of the academic journey, providing students with valuable tools and resources to excel in their studies while promoting ease of access and usability.
